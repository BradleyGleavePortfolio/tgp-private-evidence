S4 R6: revalidate the preflight owner synchronously at the notification (S4-R5-A-01)

Isolated successor to 88287cff for the frozen R5 finding S4-R5-A-01 (independent
audit A, revision 1): the preflight failure was CLASSIFIED ("not replaced")
while the run's own session was still current, but the classification travelled
back into handleStartImport / handleStartIngest across an await boundary. A
replacement whose establish was queued on the state lock could commit inside
that gap (microtask offsets 10 and 11 in the finite enumeration, both
entrypoints), so the worker broadcast auth_required / "login required to
import" while the replacement B was already current and locally valid.

reportPreflightFailure now receives the accepted Start's generation and
re-checks getSessionGeneration() synchronously, immediately before any
notification: an obsolete preflight reports the replaced detail (no
auth_required, nothing cleared); "login required" is broadcast only when the
run's OWN session is still current and could not mint. Both entrypoints pass
their admission-time generation. Nothing of the current session is read,
presented or cleared on either branch; refresh admission (shared/session.js) is
unchanged.

Regression: test/session-ownership-preflight.spec.js gains a describe block
that enumerates the replacement-commit offset (0..40 microtask turns) on both
start_import and start_ingest, asserting no auth_required is ever emitted while
a session other than the run's own is current, that the genuine OLD-current
failure remains visible exactly once, that B's refresh token is never
presented/rotated/removed, and that the single-flight guard is released; plus a
control where the replacement pairs only after the genuine report.
