## Material findings — smallest necessary closure

### S4-V3-A-01 — HIGH — shared reaper expands an unset local before declaring it

Library line **54** is `local label=$1 f=$OUT/steps/reap-$label.txt i`. The arguments of this single `local` command are expanded before its assignments take effect: `$label` is not the new `$1`. Neither native runner nor control stub initializes a preceding `label`; both use `set -u`. In the ordinary clean environment, the first `reap_owned` aborts on the unset expansion **before census or cleanup**; an inherited `label` would instead select the wrong filename. ([Library, 53–55,88](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-lib-v3.sh), [runner, 24,61,104](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-validate-v3.sh), [stub, 6,18,30](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/s4-r6-control-stub-v3.sh))

Control 1 therefore cannot establish its specified shared-reaper SUCCESS/found=1 receipt; its deliberately orphaned sleep is left to supervisor cleanup, which correctly rejects runner-left-orphans rather than satisfying that control. Native execution can perform its first setup command before encountering the same failure. **Minimal closure:** separate initialization of `label` from the dependent `f` assignment; retain the actual shared wrapper and unchanged control expectations. This declaration was carried forward from V2, not newly introduced by V3; the earlier reviews missed it. ([Stub, 26–30](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/s4-r6-control-stub-v3.sh), [predicates, 48–64](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/control-predicates-v3.py), [launcher, 133–152](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/launcher/s4-r6-launch-v3.sh), [diff, 100–103](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/V2_TO_V3.diff))

### S4-V3-A-02 — MEDIUM / MATERIAL — deferred TERM trap precedes the promised primary-step latch

Independently of A-01, the live-step control's required attribution contradicts the handler order: session-wide TERM includes runner Bash and its foreground timeout group; Bash runs the pending trap when the foreground command finishes, **before** the subsequent `local rc=$?` and required-failure latch. The stub/native handler immediately calls `block SIGNAL_TERM …`; the first-write-wins latch cannot subsequently replace that with `C-live-step`. Moving the normal command block ahead of cleanup/source blocks at library 98–100 did not put it ahead of the signal trap. ([Launcher, 41–44,118–122](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/launcher/s4-r6-launch-v3.sh), [stub, 21–22,32–36](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/s4-r6-control-stub-v3.sh), [runner, 69–71](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-validate-v3.sh), [library, 18–23,85–100](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-lib-v3.sh))

The intended interruption path therefore cannot be relied upon to satisfy predicate 75, which requires `first_failure.step=C-live-step`, not `SIGNAL_TERM`. **Minimal closure:** while a step is active, record the pending signal without first-failure latching/reentrant step cleanup, then capture and latch its actual command result before recording the secondary signal; define the no-active-step case separately. Preserve observed exit and validation exit, rather than changing the control to hide the mismatch. ([Predicates, 65–77](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/control-predicates-v3.py), [request, 65](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/SLOT_REQUEST_V3.md))

### S4-V3-A-03 — MEDIUM / MATERIAL — 185 seconds is an admission estimate, not an enforced total bound

Driver 36 checks arithmetic only **before** its synchronous launcher at 39; predicate/publication/log work at 41–49 and the final result at 59 have no enclosing elapsed deadline or cancellation/cleanup path. There is no mechanism to stop the current operation when the aggregate allowance expires. The launcher similarly finishes its runner deadline loop before final census, receipt parsing and publication; those operations are outside that deadline. This is a concrete coverage gap, not a claim that any of them has actually hung. ([Driver, 16–24,34–60](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/run-controls-v3.sh), [launcher, 112–159,49–82](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/launcher/s4-r6-launch-v3.sh))

The arithmetic also reserves **7 seconds** for reap/publication even though the post-exit orphan branch alone permits **5 + 3 = 8 one-second sleeps**, excluding census, signalling and publication; the native request says ≤22 seconds while the actual JSON emits +7. Increasing the constant alone would not enforce a hard deadline. **Minimal closure:** freeze one explicit total-deadline/cancellation route covering the active launcher and downstream receipt work, reserve cleanup time, and reconcile code/record/request budgets; cancellation must forward to and verify the detached owned session, not merely kill the driver. No general runner-framework replacement is needed. ([Launcher, 75,137–138](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/launcher/s4-r6-launch-v3.sh), [driver, 24–30](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/run-controls-v3.sh), [request, 54–56,81–82,90](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/SLOT_REQUEST_V3.md), [arithmetic](VERDICT.json))

### S4-V3-A-04 — HIGH / NATIVE ONLY — canonical lock does not cover supervisor-owned cleanup

The native runner alone opens/holds fd 9, and step children explicitly close it. The supervisor can KILL the runner as part of whole-session KILL, then confirm its exit and only afterward census/reap remaining session members; it holds no canonical lock during that interval. Thus closing the nested-PG visibility bug does **not** close the earlier requirement to retain exclusion through verified termination: a next lock holder can enter while supervisor cleanup is still pending, or after a survivor is merely reported. No such overlap was executed or observed here. ([Runner, 64–67](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-validate-v3.sh), [library, 85](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/runner/s4-r6-lib-v3.sh), [launcher, 41–44,120–141](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/launcher/s4-r6-launch-v3.sh), [prior A-01 closure requirement](../../v2/a/REPORT.md))

**Minimal closure:** retain an authoritative lock holder outside the killable workload until owned termination is verified, or terminate/verify step members before permitting the lock-holding runner to exit; an unverified survivor must prevent slot handoff. The six lock-free controls cannot attest this native lock property and should not be described as doing so. ([Stub, 2–5](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/controls/s4-r6-control-stub-v3.sh), [request, 34,60](../../../../s4-r6-validation/v3/SLOT_REQUEST_V3.md))
