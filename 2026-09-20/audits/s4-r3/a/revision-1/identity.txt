    S4 R3: bound auth body reads inside the deadline; epoch-isolate refresh coalescer
    
    The pairing redeem and token refresh paths awaited fetchWithTimeout only
    until response headers arrived, then parsed the body outside the deadline.
    A 2xx/4xx whose body stream never closes left the coach's single pairing
    submit and every coalesced refresh caller pending until browser teardown,
    and a clear + re-establish joined the stale in-flight refresh.
    
    - shared/net.js: fetchWithTimeout passes its AbortSignal to the consumer;
      new readBoundedJson reads at most MAX_AUTH_BODY_BYTES inside the deadline,
      cancels the reader on abort without awaiting a possibly hung cancel, and
      throws a byte-free BodyError. discardBody requests cancellation of bodies
      we will not read. Non-stream {ok,status,json} responses still work.
    - shared/pairing.js: consume the body inside the deadline consumer; a
      deadline abort mid-body is the existing timeout copy, not a parse error.
    - shared/session.js: refresh consumes/bounds the body inside the deadline,
      discards non-2xx bodies, keeps epoch-fenced commit under the lock; the
      in-flight coalescer is detached on clearTokens/establishSession and an old
      run's finally can no longer clear a newer slot.
    - shared/log.js: allowlist auth_body_cancel_failed (fixed code, no bytes).
    - test/auth-body-deadline.spec.js: regressions on real Response bodies over
      never-closing ReadableStreams with fake timers.
