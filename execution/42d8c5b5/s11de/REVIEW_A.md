Current new service tests merge different platforms or disjoint ids (`test/scout/roster/scout-roster.service.spec.ts:1074-1109`; `test/scout/entities/scout-entities.service.spec.ts:1547-1560`) and do not hit a same-platform/same-id cross-token tie.
