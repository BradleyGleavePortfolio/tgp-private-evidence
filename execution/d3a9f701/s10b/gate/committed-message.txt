feat(scout): S10-B run declaration and observation persistence

Adds the persistence tier of the S10 unseen-source induction decision
(D-S10-2, D-S10-4, docs/decisions/2026-09-26-s10-induction.md) on
top of the S10-A pure contract.

prisma/schema.prisma gains three insert-only models, ScoutRunDeclaration,
ScoutRunObservation and ScoutRunSettledBasis, plus the three
ScoutImport back-relations Prisma needs for their foreign keys.
No existing column changes. The expand migration
20270124000000_scout_run_observation_expand creates the tables
with forced RLS, a service_role policy and restrictive denials for
anon and authenticated, grants service_role SELECT and INSERT only,
and refuses UPDATE, top-level DELETE and TRUNCATE by trigger. One
32-byte challenge and one declared_at per run are enforced in the
database. down.sql checks the exact catalog, refuses while any row
exists and drops without CASCADE.

src/scout/induction adds the observation service, DTOs, controller
and module. POST runs/declaration and POST runs/observation run in
one transaction under FOR NO KEY UPDATE on the open server run.
Coach, epoch, challenge, received_at and the evidence digest are
bound on the server and never read from the body. Refusals reuse the
S10-A conflict codes and the existing run codes. The module is not
registered yet: ScoutModule wiring, the settle path and the importer
contract belong to S10-C, so docs/contracts/importer-openapi.json is
unchanged here.

Unit specs cover the service and controller. test/rls-g2-s10b.spec.ts
and the test/utils/g2-s10b-* harness are the S10-B real-PG proof on
a disposable marked PG 17 lane (173 migrations).
