## Finding

### B1 — J13's lock pause proves one winner and a blocked request, not **two re-drives and a loser CAS miss**

**Where:** `test/scout/s11/settle-redrive.pg.spec.ts:249–301`.

**Blocked/harm:** Worker A is paused at `locked` **after** it takes the settle-tail run-row lock. Worker B then starts `/complete`, whose *first* statement is the claim gate UPDATE. B blocks on A's lock (`g2-s11-worker.cjs:234–244`; `g2-s11-pg-harness.ts:208–220`). When A resumes and writes the terminal, B's initial gate returns zero; B takes the closed-gate acknowledgement and never reaches P2002, the second eligibility check, the pass or a terminal CAS. The test's asserted single terminal/basis and two acknowledgements would all pass in this path. Thus it cannot establish the J13 claim that two re-drives race and the loser's terminal CAS miss is silent (`docs/decisions/2026-09-26-s11-journey.md:232–234,416–417`). This is a proof defect, not evidence of a product race bug.

**Minimum fix:** Stage **both** workers past P2002, eligibility and their passes before the terminal race. For example, pause A at `before-lock` after its pass, let B reach `locked` in the settle tail, resume A and observe A blocked on B's tail lock, then resume B. Assert per-worker query order/counts showing a refused completion insert, eligibility gate, and pass in *both* workers; assert one terminal UPDATE/basis INSERT, the losing tail's terminal/epoch miss with no second write, both acknowledgements and no extra push/completed event. If the one-barrier-per-worker harness cannot distinguish that sequence, add a narrowly reviewed shared-harness barrier through the S11 harness owner rather than weakening J13. **Unblocks:** a discriminating two-process replay-race proof.
