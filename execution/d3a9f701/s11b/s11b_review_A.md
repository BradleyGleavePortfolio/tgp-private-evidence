## Finding

**B-1 — J13's purported “two concurrent re-drives” only forces a blocked initial claim gate, so one retry may never enter the P2002/re-drive branch.** The test pauses worker A at the tail's `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, after A can have reconstructed the pass; worker B is started and `pg.blocked(b.name)` merely observes a lock wait (`test/scout/s11/settle-redrive.pg.spec.ts:249-278`). The worker's `'locked'` barrier is *after* the tail lock query (`test/utils/g2-s11-worker.cjs:233-244` at the committed S11-A1 head); B's **first** `assertRunOpen` in its claim transaction can be what blocks on A's run-row lock (`src/scout/scout.service.ts:373-378`; `src/scout/lifecycle/lifecycle.service.ts:638-647`). Once A commits its terminal, B's first gate returns zero, takes `RunGateClosed` and acknowledges without reaching P2002, `redriveEpoch`, or a second settle pass (`src/scout/scout.service.ts:393-408`). The asserted one INSERT, one event and two acknowledgments all pass in that weaker schedule (`test/scout/s11/settle-redrive.pg.spec.ts:278-301`). **Harm:** a product regression that fails or duplicates when two eligible retries actually race can pass the nominal J13 live proof. **Blocks:** J13's concurrent-redrive acceptance claim, not the single-retry G1/J12 path. **Minimum fix:** force *both* workers across the P2002 branch and eligibility gate before either acquires the settle-tail lock (for example, give both a `'before-lock'` barrier and wait for both `ready` signals), then release and assert each query trace contains the refused claim, re-read gate and attempted tail; retain the one CAS/basis/event assertions. Re-run that amended live case on the attested candidate.
