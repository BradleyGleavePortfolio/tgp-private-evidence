feat(scout): S10-A pure induction contract, parsers and coverage evaluator

Pure tier of the S10 unseen-source induction decision (D-S10-1, D-S10-3,
docs/decisions/2026-09-26-s10-induction.md). No I/O, no persistence, no wiring:

- contract.ts: induction manifest, declaration and observation evidence types,
  limits (statement <= 1024 bytes, observation body <= 32 KiB) and rejection codes.
- parse.ts: total, fail-closed parsers for manifests and signed evidence
  (strict base64, fatal UTF-8, byte-identical canonical JSON, exact keys) and the
  pure body-size check.
- digest.ts: canonical JSON, mapping-spec digest and the length-prefixed
  identity-set digest.
- manifest-registry.ts: load-time validated registry of approved verifier keys.
- verify.ts: coverage evaluator. Every failure yields known:false; a missing or
  conflicting staged entry never becomes zero; multiscope platforms fail closed.

Tests cover R20-R28 with synthetic fixtures under test/fixtures/scout/s10_pure
(test-only keys). The production grouping half of R27 and the terminal half of
R23 are proved in S10-C. Routes, tables and module registration belong to S10-B/C.
